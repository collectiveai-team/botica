# Credits

The deep-module architecture rules adapt concepts and examples from
[Matt Pocock](https://github.com/mattpocock)'s
[`codebase-design` skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/v1.3.1/skills/engineering/codebase-design/SKILL.md)
in `mattpocock/skills`.

This contribution covers the module/interface/implementation vocabulary,
depth as leverage and locality, the deletion test, adapter discipline, and
testing through the same interface callers use. The individual `arch-*` rule
files retain their source references.

The house toolchain, package and stack-specific rules, CES integration, and
400/700-line review thresholds are local standards, not attributed to Pocock.
